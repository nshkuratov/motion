# Afterglow — third world

The third chapter connects the first two worlds through peach lighting, plum shadows, oversized sculptural forms and the same three-line type composition. Cloud nine introduces the idea; Otherworld changes its perspective; Afterglow carries it into a lasting impression: “Make waves. Leave a mark.”

Three original assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool and saved in this workspace:

| Layer | Shipping asset | Source artwork | Exact generation prompt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lavender sea and sunset sky | [ocean-background.webp](../public/images/ocean-background.webp) | [PNG](../artwork/ocean-background.png) | [Prompt](ocean-background-prompt.txt) |
| Floating opalescent glass sculpture | [glass-knot.webp](../public/images/glass-knot.webp) | [PNG](../artwork/glass-knot.png) | [Prompt](glass-knot-prompt.txt) |
| Foreground wave | [ocean-foreground.webp](../public/images/ocean-foreground.webp) | [PNG](../artwork/ocean-foreground.png) | [Prompt](ocean-foreground-prompt.txt) |

The sculpture and wave have native alpha transparency, preserved in their WebP versions. The three new WebP files total approximately 694 KB. Their `.webp.json` sidecars retain the generation prompts.

The desert-to-ocean transition follows the first transition's scroll length and timing. The moon rises away, the background dissolves, the glass sculpture rises and rotates into place, and the foreground wave enters on a separate track. Text and caption tracks remain independent. Navigation targets the settled positions of the same shared timeline; reduced motion shows three static sections.
